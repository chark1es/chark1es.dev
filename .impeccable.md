# Design context — chark1es.dev

## Audience

Recruiters, hiring managers, and peers reviewing a CS student’s work.

## Use cases

- Quickly understand who Charles is and what he’s built
- Skim experience and projects, optionally read blog posts
- Contact or view resume

## Brand personality

Personal, calm, credible — editorial minimal. Warm paper in light mode, soft ink charcoal in dark. One muted accent for links and focus. No gimmicks, no template-card grids.

## Typography

- Plus Jakarta Sans (single sans family, all UI)

## Color

Semantic tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css` (`bg`, `surface`, `text`, `text-muted`, `border`, `accent`). OKLCH-tinted neutrals; no pure black/white or cyan-purple gradients.
