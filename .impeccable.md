# Design context

See PRODUCT.md for audience and goals, DESIGN.md for the system.

Direction: a dark, playful portfolio themed entirely around blobatars. One big
character on the home page, a cast of friends who double as navigation, flat
pastel colour taken from the creatures, Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree. It should
read as made by a person: specific first-person copy, slightly crooked stickers,
creatures with moods, nothing that looks like a template.
