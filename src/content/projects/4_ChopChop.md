An expense-splitting app for friends, roommates, and small groups. Track who owes who, add and edit expenses, and scan receipts. It ships as an Expo iOS/Android app, a public site, and an internal admin console, all sharing one Convex backend.

It handles Apple Sign-In, multi-currency balances, receipt scanning, and its own release pipeline. Every avatar in it is a blobatar, the same deterministic faces you've been poking on this site.
